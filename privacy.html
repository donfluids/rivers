Privacy policy

Rivers Research LLC · In force from 4 October 2026

This policy covers the apps published by Rivers Research LLC: Bible and വേദപുസ്തകം, the Malayalam edition of Bible. In short, the apps do not collect personal data, do not send anything from your phone, and do not use accounts, analytics or advertising.

What the apps collect

The apps collect nothing. They contain no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code. They do not ask you to sign in, and they do not connect to any server run by Rivers Research LLC.

What stays on your phone

The complete text, dictionaries and concordance are included in the app, so reading and searching need no connection. Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, marked passages, reading position and settings are stored only on the phone. On Android they are included in your phone's backup, so they are restored when you move to a new phone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing from the Bible app

When you copy or share a verse, the text goes to the clipboard or to the app you pick. Rivers Research LLC does not see it.

Permissions

The apps request no permissions.

Children

The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If an app ever starts to collect or send data, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Rivers Research LLC by email.
