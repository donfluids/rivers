Privacy policy

Rivers Research LLC · In force from 4 October 2026

This policy covers the apps published by Rivers Research LLC: Bible, വേദപുസ്തകം (the Malayalam edition of Bible) and LiveKey. The short version: the apps do not collect personal data, do not send anything off the phone on their own, and have no accounts, analytics or advertising.

What the apps collect

Nothing. The apps contain no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code of any kind. They do not ask you to sign in and they do not connect to any server run by Rivers Research LLC. There is no server.

What stays on your phone

Bible and വേദപുസ്തകം. The complete text, dictionaries and concordance ship inside the app, so reading and searching need no connection. Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, marked passages, reading position and settings are stored only on the phone. On Android they are included in the phone's own backup, so they are restored when you move to a new phone; the Bible database itself is excluded from that backup because the app already contains it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

LiveKey. The app needs the microphone to hear the music. Audio is analysed on the phone in real time and is not stored, unless you choose to record a clip (below). The microphone is released whenever the app leaves the foreground. Your choice of preset and theme is stored on the phone.

Clips you record in LiveKey

LiveKey can record clips of up to sixty seconds so that its presets can be tuned to a particular room. Recording only happens when you choose it from the menu, and a recording indicator is shown while it runs. Because a clip records everyone within earshot, let the people singing know before you record.

  • Clips are kept in the app's private storage, where other apps cannot read them.
  • Each clip is a WAV file plus a small text file holding the preset, the app's own decisions over time, and any key, tempo or notes you typed in. It records the Android version but not the phone model.
  • Nothing is uploaded. A clip leaves the phone only if you choose Share clips, and then it goes wherever you send it.
  • Delete clips removes them. Uninstalling the app removes them too.

Sharing from the Bible app

When you copy or share a verse, the text goes to the clipboard or to the app you pick. Rivers Research LLC does not see it.

Permissions

  • Bible and വേദപുസ്തകം request no permissions.
  • LiveKey requests the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO), for the reasons above, and nothing else.

Children

The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If an app ever starts to collect or send data, this page will say so before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Rivers Research LLC at dond@riversresearch.org.
