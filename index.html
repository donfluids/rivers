Numerical simulation of fluids, shock waves, blast and impact.

Problem classes

Turbulence

Turbulent flow resolved directly, including the transport and mixing of particles and scalars.

Blast and detonation

Blast loading in the open, in enclosed rooms and between buildings, and the structure of detonation fronts.

Shock and multiphase flow

Shock waves interacting with gas and liquid interfaces, including bubble collapse near walls.

Free-surface flow, impact and porous media

Wave impact on structures, deformation of metals at high rates, and flow through porous materials.

Validation and reporting procedure

Reference case

A method is first applied to a problem with a known answer from the literature.

Grid convergence

Each case is computed at more than one resolution, and quantities that have not converged are identified.

Reported deviation

Every result is stated with its difference from the reference, including where the agreement is weaker.

Solvers used: OpenFOAM, blastFoam, MFC, OpenRadioss, HIT36 DNS, RECUR

Contract simulation and analysis

Simulation studies

Problem setup, computation and analysis for a specified engineering question.

Structural loading

Pressure, impulse and force histories on walls, facades and obstacles under blast, wave impact or flow.

Model validation

Reproduction of a published experiment with the client's solver and settings. Agreement and disagreement are both reported.

Figures and animation

Publication figures and rendered animation produced from the computed fields.
