Bible

AndroidiOS

The King James Version and the World English Bible, with the Hebrew and Greek underneath every word. Tap an underlined word to see the original, its grammar in plain English, a short meaning, the pronunciation and Strong's dictionary entry. You can also list every verse that uses the same Hebrew or Greek word.

  • Hebrew and Greek text of every verse, word by word, with transliteration and gloss
  • Highlights in four colors, notes and bookmarks, including passages that span several verses
  • Search the text or the dictionary, or type a reference such as John 3:16 or a Strong's number such as G26
  • Poetry keeps its line breaks, and footnotes and cross references open with a tap
  • Light, sepia and dark themes, with adjustable text size
  • The full text and dictionary are included, so it works offline

വേദപുസ്തകം

AndroidiOSMalayalam

The same app in Malayalam, with the 1910 Sathyavedapusthakam alongside the King James Version. Each Malayalam word is linked to the Hebrew or Greek behind it, so you can tap a word and study it in Malayalam.

  • Sathyavedapusthakam 1910, the King James Version, and the Hebrew and Greek interlinear
  • The Malayalam word shown next to the original, with its English meaning
  • References accept everyday spellings and Catholic book names
  • Search finds the 1910 text using modern Malayalam spelling
  • Interface in Malayalam, with English available
  • Can be installed alongside the English app

LiveKey

Android

For keyboard players who need to join in after the congregation has started singing. Tap Listen and hold the phone toward the singing. LiveKey shows the key, the current chord, the tempo and the time signature, usually within a few seconds.

  • The key, the chords that belong to it, and how far the singers are from standard pitch
  • A running list of the chords in the song, as letters or Nashville numbers
  • Hold keeps the key on screen while you play, and Transpose adjusts it for your keyboard
  • Tempo in beats per minute, with a beat counter
  • Settings for a full band, a guitar or voices only
  • Listening happens on the phone, and no audio is sent anywhere
Android 8.0 and up
About

About us

Rivers Research LLC is an independent company that builds and publishes the apps on this page. We started making them for our own Bible study and for our own church services.

Our apps do not collect personal information, show ads or require an account. Because they work offline, you can use them anywhere, including in a church with poor reception.

Works offlineYour notes and highlights are saved on your phone and included in its regular backup.
PrivateWe do not collect any data from our apps. Our privacy policy explains the details.
Trusted sourcesThe apps include the complete King James Version, World English Bible and 1910 Sathyavedapusthakam. The Hebrew and Greek text comes from the Leningrad Codex and STEPBible. Each app lists its sources in its settings.
